Clade Privacy Policy

Last updated November 27, 2026

This policy explains what the Clade app collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. Clade is made by OTB Industries, LLC ("OTB", "we", "us"), a New York company.

The short version

  • We collect only what Clade needs to work: your account, your library and your progress.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or tracking.
  • Quizzes are written and graded by a third-party AI service. Clade sends it paper text and your written answers only after you agree, and you can turn this off any time.
  • Questions you ask the Ask AI assistant go to the same AI service, also only after you agree.
  • Messages you exchange with a paper's authors through Ask the authors are private to you and that paper's authors. Authors prove who they are by linking their ORCID iD.
  • You can delete your account and everything tied to it from Profile in the app.

What we collect

Your account

You sign in with Apple or Google. Either one gives us a stable identifier for your account, and your name and email address. With Apple you can choose not to share your name, and if you choose "Hide My Email", we get an Apple relay address instead of your real one. With Google we keep your email only if Google has verified it. An Apple account and a Google account are separate Clade accounts, even with the same email. We also keep the date you joined, your membership or trial status, and whether you have agreed to Clade's AI features.

What you do in Clade

  • The research fields and topics you pick as interests.
  • Your library: papers you save, start or finish, the ratings you give them, and the reasons a paper was suggested.
  • Your quiz results, XP, streak and titles.
  • Your most recent paper suggestions, so Clade can still show them if a refresh fails.

PDFs you upload

When you upload a paper's PDF, we pull out its text and then discard the PDF. We don't keep the file. A quiz written from it is saved under a private code that only your library holds, so only you can open it. We also keep the text privately for you, so the Ask AI assistant can answer your questions from it; other readers never see it. Deleting your account removes both.

Questions you ask the AI assistant

Ask AI answers your questions about a paper. Each time you ask, Clade sends the AI service your question, your earlier messages in that chat, and the paper's text. It works only after you agree on the AI consent card, and the AI service does not receive your name, email, account or library. We keep your Ask AI chats so you can pick them up later, and only you can see them. Clear chat deletes one, and deleting your account deletes them all.

Messages with paper authors

Ask the authors lets you send a private question about a paper to its authors, and lets authors reply. We store the chat, the paper it is about, and the name each person shows. You can ask anonymously, and then authors see "A Clade reader" instead of your name. A chat is visible only to you and to the paper's authors who have linked their ORCID iD on Clade; other readers never see it. These chats are not end-to-end encrypted: we can read a message when it is reported to us, or when we need to for safety, security or the law.

To keep chats civil, every message passes an automatic word filter. If the sender has agreed to Clade's AI features, the message also gets a quick automatic check by the AI service for harassment, threats and spam. We also keep the reports and blocks you make.

Your ORCID iD, if you're an author

Authors link their ORCID iD by signing in at orcid.org. ORCID sends us only your iD and the name on your ORCID record; we never see your ORCID password. We use them to show you questions about papers that list you as an author, and to show your name on your replies. You can unlink your iD any time.

Feedback emails

The Send feedback button opens an email in your mail app. It fills in the app version, your phone model, its operating system version and your Clade account number so we can track down problems. You can edit or remove any of it before sending. We keep feedback emails to reply and to improve Clade.

Technical data

Like most online services, our servers record basic request information, such as IP address, time and the address requested, to keep Clade running and secure. These logs are kept for a short time.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos, or any data from other apps, and we don't track you across apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run your account and keep your library and progress in sync across your devices.
  • To suggest papers, write and grade quizzes, and answer your questions with Ask AI.
  • To deliver messages between readers and authors, and to keep those chats safe.
  • To manage your trial or membership.
  • To answer you when you contact us, and to fix problems and improve Clade.
  • To keep Clade secure and to meet legal obligations.

Who we share it with

We share data only with the service providers that run Clade for us, and only what each one needs. They may use it only to provide their service to us.

  • A third-party AI service, currently Anthropic's Claude, writes and grades quizzes and answers Ask AI questions. Before your first quiz or question, Clade asks for your permission to send it the text of each paper you take a quiz on or ask about (including PDFs you upload), your written answers, your questions to the assistant, and the messages you send to authors, for a quick safety check. It does not receive your name, email, Apple or Google account, library or progress. We use it under business terms that don't let it train its models on what we send. You can withdraw permission any time on Profile, under AI quizzes and Ask AI.
  • ORCID confirms an author's iD when they link it. Clade sends ORCID nothing about readers.
  • Other Clade users see only what you send them in Ask the authors: your messages, and your name unless you ask anonymously.
  • Hosting and database providers run our servers and store the data described above.
  • Our app platform provider delivers app updates to your phone, which means it receives basic technical information such as your IP address and device type.
  • Apple handles Sign in with Apple, and will handle payments once memberships are on sale. We never see your payment card.
  • Google handles Sign in with Google. Clade asks Google only for your account identifier, name and email, and never sees your Google password.

Paper suggestions come from OpenAlex, a free index of research papers. Clade looks up papers by topic and citation; it doesn't send OpenAlex anything that identifies you.

We may also disclose data if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or merger of OTB, in which case this policy continues to apply to your data. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we don't share it for advertising.

How long we keep it

We keep your account data until you delete your account. "Start over" on Profile clears your library, progress and private quizzes but keeps your account. Deleting your account removes your profile, library, ratings, quiz results, XP, streak, private quizzes, saved suggestions, Ask AI chats and the author chats you started from our database right away. Replies you sent as an author stay in the readers' chats they answer, without a link to your account. Our providers' backup copies expire within 30 days. Sign-in sessions expire on their own. Feedback emails are kept until they are no longer needed.

Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account: Profile, then Delete account.
  • Turn off AI features: Profile, then AI quizzes and Ask AI, then Turn off. This stops quizzes and Ask AI from sending anything to the AI service.
  • Clear an Ask AI chat: open the chat, then Clear chat.
  • Report or block someone: under any message from someone else in Ask the authors.
  • Unlink your ORCID iD: Profile, then Unlink ORCID.
  • Stop Sign in with Apple: on your iPhone, Settings, your name, Sign in with Apple, Clade.
  • Stop Sign in with Google: at myaccount.google.com, Security, Your connections to third-party apps and services, Clade.
  • Get a copy or a correction: email us and we'll send a copy of your data or fix anything that's wrong.

Depending on where you live, including California and the European Economic Area, you may have further rights, such as to object to or restrict how we use your data, or to complain to your local data protection authority. Email us to use any of them; we'll answer within 30 days and won't treat you differently for asking. Where EU or UK law applies, we rely on our contract with you to run your account, on your consent for AI features, and on our legitimate interest in keeping Clade secure and working.

Security

Data travels encrypted between the app and our servers. Your sign-in token is kept in your phone's secure storage, and we store only a scrambled (hashed) copy of it. No system is perfectly secure, but we limit what we collect and who can reach it.

Children

Clade is for researchers and students aged 13 and over (16 and over in the European Economic Area). We don't knowingly collect data from younger children. If you believe a child has given us data, email us and we'll delete it.

Where data is stored

Our servers are in the United States. If you use Clade from elsewhere, your data is transferred to and processed in the United States.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we'll tell you in the app before it takes effect.
