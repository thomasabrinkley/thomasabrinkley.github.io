Small, careful software for people who learn for a living.

OTB Industries is an independent software company. Our first app is Clade.

Read papers. Prove you understood them.

Clade suggests research papers that fit what you study, writes a short quiz for each one you read, and tracks your progress as you work from a field's foundations to its frontier, following how papers cite each other.

  • Suggestions built from your interests, your library and the citation graph
  • Quizzes on any paper, including PDFs you upload
  • XP, streaks and titles that grow as you master a topic

Clade is in a private beta for iPhone. Public launch on the App Store is planned for early 2027.
