Common questions

How do I sign in?

Tap Sign in with Apple or Sign in with Google on the first screen. With Apple you can choose to hide your email; Clade works the same either way. An Apple account and a Google account are separate Clade accounts, even with the same email, so use the same one each time.

Why don't I see suggestions on Today?

Suggestions need an internet connection and at least one interest. Check your connection, then make sure you've picked interests (Profile, then Edit interests). If Clade can't refresh, it shows your last suggestions with a note until it can.

How do quizzes work?

Open a paper and start its quiz. Before your first quiz, Clade asks permission to send the paper's text and your written answers to the AI service that writes and grades quizzes. If a paper's full text isn't freely available, you can upload your own PDF of it.

How do I turn off AI quizzes?

Profile, then AI quizzes, then Turn off. Nothing more is sent to the AI service. You can turn it back on from the same place.

How do I start over?

Profile, then Start over. This clears your library, XP, streak, interests and private quizzes but keeps your account.

How do I delete my account?

Profile, then Delete account, then confirm. This permanently deletes your account and everything saved with it: your library, ratings, quiz results, XP, streak and private quizzes. It can't be undone. If you can't open the app, email us from the address you signed in with and we'll delete it for you.

How do I cancel a membership?

Memberships are billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Clade. Deleting your account doesn't cancel an Apple subscription, so cancel it there first. For refunds, use Apple's Report a Problem page.

Does Clade work on Android?

Not yet. Clade is iPhone only for now.
